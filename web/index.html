Interface games — Figure 14

The flow field shows where selection pushes every population mix at the current cost (arrows), with a ring on the attractor it converges to. Click (or drag) inside the triangle to seed a starting mix and press Play to send a population riding along the flow. Low cost → truth wins; past ~4.27% → the interface (IF3) wins. A strategy seeded at 0% can never enter. Toggle the arrows to switch between the field and a clean trajectory.
